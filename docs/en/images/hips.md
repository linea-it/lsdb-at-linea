# HiPS

**[HiPS](https://aladin.cds.unistra.fr/hips/)** (Hierarchical Progressive Surveys) images enable **global visualization** and **navigation** through the [Sky Viewer](https://skyviewer.org/) application, or pointing to a pre-selected list of targets in the [Target Viewer](https://targetviewer.linea.org.br/).

The HiPS format organizes astronomical images into progressive resolution levels, making sky exploration efficient at different scales. Official documentation is available at [aladin.cds.unistra.fr/hips](https://aladin.cds.unistra.fr/hips/).

Catalogs can also be visualized overlaid on HiPS images. For a summary of the available datasets, see the [Images](index.md) section and the [home page](../index.md).
