# LSST DP1 FITS products

The **Legacy Survey of Space and Time Data Preview 1 (LSST DP1)** contains image and catalog products generated by Rubin Science Pipelines v29 from real LSST Commissioning Camera (ComCam) observations of seven fields over seven weeks in 2024. The official release date was June 30, 2025.

- [Official DP1 documentation](https://dp1.lsst.io/)
- [DP1 image products](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/index.html)
- [Official dataset citation](https://doi.org/10.71929/rubin/2570308)

## Access level

!!! private "Restricted access"
    DP1 is proprietary and available only to Rubin data rights holders, under the [Rubin Data Policy](https://rubinobservatory.org/for-scientists/data-products/data-policy) and [RDO-013](https://docushare.lsst.org/docushare/dsweb/Get/RDO-013).

## Products available at LIneA

## Sky coverage by band

DP1 observed seven fields of approximately one square degree each. Coverage is not the same across bands:

- **u:** ECDFS, EDFS, Low Galactic Latitude Field, and Seagull Nebula.
- **g and r:** all seven fields.
- **i:** all fields except Seagull Nebula.
- **z:** Low Ecliptic Latitude Field, ECDFS, EDFS, Low Galactic Latitude Field, and Seagull Nebula.
- **y:** 47 Tuc, ECDFS, EDFS, and Low Galactic Latitude Field.

The table gives each field center and the official number of visits per band. Zero means that the field was not observed in that band.

!!! info "How to interpret coverage"
    These figures describe visits observed by Rubin. Deep coadds include only exposures that meet quality criteria; edge patches may contain areas without data, marked by the `NO_DATA` mask. Local file counts by band are listed in the product tables below.

Official sources: [observations and filters](https://dp1.lsst.io/overview/observations.html), [image quality and depth](https://dp1.lsst.io/overview/iqsummary.html), and [coadd processing](https://dp1.lsst.io/processing/coaddition/index.html).

### Deep coadd

A band-by-band combination of calibrated, background-subtracted exposures for a sky region, using an inverse-variance-weighted mean. Each file represents a patch in one of the six `u`, `g`, `r`, `i`, `z`, and `y` bands.

- **Format:** FITS (`.fits`)
- **Local inventory:** 2,654 files, 166 GB
- **Contents:** image (nJy), variance (nJy²), and mask planes, plus PSF, photometric calibration, and WCS metadata
- **Official documentation:** [Deep coadd](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/deep_coadd.html), [image coaddition](https://dp1.lsst.io/processing/coaddition/index.html), and [mask planes](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/deep_coadd_mask_planes.html)

| Band | Files | Size |
|---|---:|---:|
| u | 274 | 16 GB |
| g | 583 | 37 GB |
| r | 581 | 39 GB |
| i | 510 | 32 GB |
| z | 432 | 28 GB |
| y | 263 | 15 GB |

### Deep coadd background

### Deep coadd n-image

- **Local inventory:** 2,721 files, 1.7 GB
- **Related documentation:** [Deep coadd](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/images/deep_coadd.html)

### HealSparse FITS maps

Twelve survey-property maps, one file per band (`u`, `g`, `r`, `i`, `z`, `y`), totaling 72 files and 451 MB. They use FITS with the `.hsp` extension.

- **Official documentation:** [Survey property maps](https://dp1.lsst.io/products/spmaps/index.html)

| Product | Description |
|---|---|
| `deepCoadd_dcr_ddec_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean DCR displacement in declination |
| `deepCoadd_dcr_dra_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean DCR displacement in right ascension |
| `deepCoadd_dcr_e1_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean DCR distortion component `e1` |
| `deepCoadd_dcr_e2_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean DCR distortion component `e2` |
| `deepCoadd_epoch_consolidated_map_max` | Maximum epoch of contributing exposures |
| `deepCoadd_epoch_consolidated_map_mean` | Mean epoch of contributing exposures |
| `deepCoadd_epoch_consolidated_map_min` | Minimum epoch of contributing exposures |
| `deepCoadd_psf_e1_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean PSF component `e1` |
| `deepCoadd_psf_e2_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean PSF component `e2` |
| `deepCoadd_psf_size_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean PSF size |
| `deepCoadd_sky_background_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean sky-background level |
| `deepCoadd_sky_noise_consolidated_map_weighted_mean` | Weighted mean sky noise |

## Inventory limits

This page covers only FITS products currently documented in LIneA's local copy. Official DP1 also provides raw exposures, visit images, template coadds, and difference images that are not recorded as available in these local directories.
